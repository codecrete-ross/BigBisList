# Big BiS List 0.15.0

Addon behavior changes in the 0.15.x releases, newest first. Each section describes changes introduced in that version.

## 0.15.0 - 2026-11-18

- Added earlier-tier recommendations for catch-up gearing, enabled by default in Upgrades and optional in BiS List, while keeping the selected tier as the gearing goal.
- Restored directly clickable tier choices with a Target tier label and a separate Live marker.
- Added Compare slot with equipped, bag, and cached bank items, selectable references, local specialization choices including Cat DPS and Bear Tank, and recommendations from Pre-Raid through Sunwell. Returning restores the original results and position.
- Compare slot stays accessible while scrolling Details. Comparison lists support guide-based ordering, sortable columns, and an Owned gear filter.
- Guide comparisons now explain higher, lower, equal, setup-dependent, or unresolved ratings and identify the supporting tier. Source-table positions no longer imply item strength.
- Replaced Upgrades only with Upgrade candidates, Higher guide rating, and All recommendations, preserving existing workspace preferences.
- Details, Wishlist, and tooltips now show specific later recommendation roles, including gaps and reappearances, without treating an absent recommendation as an expiration date.
- Improved equipment comparisons for unranked gear, paired slots, duplicate equipped items, two-handed weapons, and supported alternatives in bags or cached bank contents.
- Details now shows hoverable acquisition tokens and quest-starter items, their required quantities, and recorded drop rates for each source. Unknown rates remain explicit, and long source lists use paging.
- Corrected the Naaru Belt of Precision quest rewards to appear under their required Botanica and Mechanar activities instead of the Tempest Keep raid filter.
- Reorganized the gearing workspaces around Upgrades, By Slot, Gear Guide, and Equipped. Use the equipment slot picker to browse a specific slot and the Upgrades view to see prioritized actions.
- The spec and level selectors resume following your character when the selected value matches the live value. The level selector keeps a stable label while showing your chosen preview level.

### WoW Forever

- Added a separate Forever client build with isolated saves and race- and faction-aware Leveling and level-60 Pre-Raid recommendations for 28 profiles.
- Leveling groups obtainable gear by activity and shows next quest steps. Acquisition details show quest progress, eligibility, and separate routes, with waypoint actions for verified Forever destinations.
- Gear Guide and item tooltips use the same reference ranks for each spec, activity, and level. Verified faction routes determine which items enter the pool. Common and Poor gear can earn a rank.
- Item tooltips mark ties, projected exact rolls, and unresolved effects. Above-level items show estimates against the best gear you already own at the first usable level, including items in your cached bank.
- Compare Slot labels ranks for the equipped setup. Details shows tier ranges, the first level a stronger option appears, and the largest stat gains and losses. Current swap estimates can use active talents where supported.
- Quest reward tooltips show which choices serve each spec and activity. Acquisition badges highlight upgrades in loot, quest rewards, auctions, bags, and bank. A quest with no gear upgrade can mark its highest-value vendor reward.
- The Forever minimap button works with Leatrix Plus button collection.
